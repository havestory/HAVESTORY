10. Final Instructions for the UI Designer
Redesign the visual interface only.
Do not remove, rename or simplify existing business functions without approval.
Do not add a Smart Print Calculator.
Keep all Settings-driven values dynamic.
Do not hardcode HAVESTORY contact or business information.
Provide desktop and mobile designs.
Include empty, loading, error, success and confirmation states.
Include drawers, modals, tables, cards, forms and print layouts.
Use consistent spacing, typography, buttons, status badges and form fields.
Make daily admin operations quicker than the current interface.
Use photography and framing terminology rather than generic printing-company terminology.
Preserve offers, coupons, products, services, orders, CRM, invoices, finance, inventory, shipping, staff and website-management capabilities.
Use a completely original HAVESTORY visual system.
